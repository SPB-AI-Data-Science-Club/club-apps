Boosting, One Tiny Tree at a Time

Boosting builds a team of weak learners where each new stump only tries to fix what the team got wrong so far. Add trees and watch the green fit snap onto the data as the leftover error, the residual, shrinks. Race to get the training error under the target line in the fewest trees. Add too many and the test error starts creeping back up, which is overfitting sneaking in.
