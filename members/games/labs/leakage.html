The Leakage Heist

You are predicting which students pass the class. Pick the features to train on, then hit train. One of these features is a trap that quietly contains the answer. It will rocket your training accuracy toward a hundred percent, and then the honest hold out set, data the model has never seen, will expose the whole thing. Find the best score you can get honestly.

Target leakage is one of the most common ways a real model looks amazing in testing and then fails in production. The fix is boring but vital, only feed the model information it would truly have at the moment of prediction.
